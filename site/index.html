RESEARCH GATE

What a real study needs

  1. Licensed corpus and independent relevance judgments.
  2. Frozen cases, splits, budgets, and ranking metrics.
  3. Strong fixed baselines and statistical comparison.
  4. Error analysis and clinical review where appropriate.
